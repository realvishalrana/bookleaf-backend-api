# BookLeaf Knowledge Base

## Royalty & Payments

Royalty payouts are processed quarterly for balances above INR 2,500. Authors receive 70% of net sales after platform and distribution fees. Payouts are transferred to the bank account on file within 7–10 business days after the quarterly cycle closes.

If you have not received a payout in 6+ months, verify: (1) your bank details in the author portal, (2) whether pending balance exceeds the minimum threshold, and (3) whether sales reports have been reconciled for the period. Escalate to finance if pending royalty exceeds INR 5,000 and last payout was over 90 days ago.

Royalty statements are available in your author dashboard. Discrepancies should include book title, ISBN, and the sales period in question.

## ISBN & Metadata Issues

ISBN assignment takes 5–7 business days after final manuscript approval. Corrections to title, subtitle, or author name on retail listings require a metadata update request and may take 2–4 weeks to propagate across Amazon, Flipkart, and other channels.

If your ISBN appears incorrect on a retailer page, share the retailer URL and the correct metadata. BookLeaf will submit correction requests to our distribution partners.

## Printing & Quality

Print quality issues (binding, color, paper defects) should include order batch if known and photos where possible. Reprints for confirmed defects are arranged at no cost to the author. Standard print partners include In-House, Repro India, and Epitome Books.

## Distribution & Availability

New titles typically appear on Amazon India within 2–3 weeks of going live. International expansion (Amazon US/UK) depends on rights and genre; timelines vary 4–8 weeks. Out-of-stock on a platform is often a retailer inventory sync issue—BookLeaf can request a refresh from our distributor.

## Book Status & Production Updates

Production stages: Manuscript Review → Editing → Cover Design → Typesetting → Print Proof → Published & Live. Authors receive email updates at stage changes. In-production titles do not accrue sales royalties until status is Published & Live.

## General Inquiry

For account profile updates (bio, photo, contact), use the author portal or reply to your onboarding email. Response time for non-urgent inquiries is 2–3 business days.
